Privacy Policy & Security Architecture

Application: DocuSnap AI Finder (com.docusnap.ai)
Effective Date: November 18, 2026

💡 Solving The World's Biggest Gallery Frustration

Every smartphone owner takes thousands of photos: medicine prescriptions, tax bills, grocery receipts, bank slips, warranties, and WhatsApp screenshots. Over months, these pile up into a massive maze of 40,000+ unorganized gallery images.

When standing at a clinic counter, airport, or pharmacy, finding that one specific receipt takes 20 stressful minutes of manual scrolling—or is lost forever. DocuSnap AI turns this into a game of seconds. If you remember just ONE single word from that image (e.g. "Pharmaprix", "Kenwood", "Augmentin", or "14820"), DocuSnap AI’s instant on-device OCR retrieves the exact visual snapshot in milliseconds.

Because your gallery contains your most private documents, DocuSnap AI was engineered from day one with a strict Zero-Surveillance, Privacy-First Architecture. Here is how your data is protected.

🔒 100% On-Device OCR

Text indexing runs directly on your smartphone processor via Google ML Kit. Standard photos are never transmitted to external servers for OCR.

📦 Zero Storage Duplication

We read existing MediaStore paths directly. DocuSnap AI never makes unwanted duplicate copies that clutter your device storage.

🛡️ AES-256 Cloud Vault

Synced document text and thumbnails are isolated behind per-user Firebase security rules. Nobody else can ever view your vault.

1

Information We Collect & Process

DocuSnap AI is designed to minimize data collection strictly to what is required to make your documents searchable:

  • Document Images & Screenshots: When granted permission (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, CAMERA, DETECT_SCREEN_CAPTURE), our lightweight scanner identifies receipts, utility bills, prescriptions, and text-bearing captures in your gallery. Small icons (<150px) and non-document photos are automatically ignored.
  • Extracted OCR Text & Metadata: Words recognized in receipts, dates, item names, and totals are extracted into your private on-device SQLite FTS5 database to enable instant search.
  • Account Credentials: If you choose to enable cross-device cloud sync, we utilize Google Sign-In or Firebase Anonymous Authentication to generate a secure, unique User ID (UID). We do not collect passwords or phone contacts.
  • Device & Telemetry Data: Minimal diagnostic information (such as app version, OS level, and crash logs via Firebase Crashlytics) to ensure stability across Android 8 through Android 16.
2

How We Use Your Data

Your document data is processed solely for your own benefit:

  • Instant Multi-term Search: To enable you to type any keyword and instantly view matched receipts, prescriptions, and bills.
  • Cross-Platform Remote Web Access: Allowing you to securely access your vault from your laptop or PC browser at docusnap.consultways.com when away from your mobile device.
  • AI Entity Extraction (Optional): Deep medical and expense reasoning (parsing medicine doses or bill breakdown) is powered securely via Google Gemini API through encrypted backend endpoints. Your private documents are never used to train public AI models.
3

Google Play Billing & Subscriptions

DocuSnap AI offers flexible storage tiers (Starter Pro, Silver Vault, Gold Clinic, Platinum Enterprise, and Infinity Diamond) as well as prepaid lifetime passes:

  • Zero Financial Retention: All subscription charges and in-app purchases are processed directly by Google Play Billing (Play Billing Library 8.3.0). DocuSnap AI never sees, stores, or transmits your credit card numbers, debit cards, or banking credentials.
  • Subscription Management: Subscriptions renew automatically per the plan chosen (monthly). You can cancel, pause, or change your subscription at any time directly in the Google Play Store under Play Store > Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.

5

Security & Encryption Standards

We deploy defense-in-depth security to ensure zero unauthorized access:

  • Data In-Transit: All data transmitted between your device, the web portal, and Firebase servers uses TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption.
  • Data At-Rest: Synced cloud documents and thumbnails are stored in Google Cloud Firestore & Storage with AES-256 encryption.
  • Strict Cloud Access Rules: Firestore security rules guarantee that queries to /users/{userId}/documents/* are authorized only when request.auth.uid == userId. No other user can query or view your documents.
6

User Rights & Data Deletion (GDPR & CCPA)

You retain full sovereignty over your documents and account at all times:

  • 1-Click Document Deletion: Deleting a document from your mobile app or web portal immediately removes it from your local SQLite database and permanently deletes the synced record and cloud thumbnail.
  • Complete Account Deletion: You can request full purge of your account, UID, and all synced data anytime by clicking "Delete Account" in Settings or by emailing support@consultways.com.
  • Offline-Only Mode: If you wish to disable cloud synchronization completely, simply switch the app to "On-Device OCR Only" in Settings. The app will continue indexing and searching your receipts 100% offline.
