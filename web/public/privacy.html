Privacy Policy for DocuSnap AI

Effective Date: November 11, 2026  |  App Package: com.docusnap.ai

1. Overview & Local-First Architecture

DocuSnap AI ("DocuSnap", "we", "our") is built with an Offline-First & Privacy-First architecture to organize medical prescriptions, expense receipts, utility bills, and important screenshots securely.

2. Permissions & Data Processing

  • Photos, Camera & Screenshot Capture: With your permission (CAMERA, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, DETECT_SCREEN_CAPTURE), DocuSnap AI saves your camera scans and screenshots locally in full resolution to your on-device Smart Vault. Small low-resolution icons (< 150px) and non-document photos without text (when not captured as screenshots/scans) are skipped automatically.
  • On-Device OCR: Standard Optical Character Recognition runs 100% locally on your device using Google ML Kit without uploading images externally.
  • Global Multilingual AI Search & Vision (Optional 5x Feature): When you have an active prepaid balance and use Global Multilingual AI Search or Vision extraction, encrypted HTTPS requests are sent to our Firebase Cloud Functions backend to parse document entities in your requested language via Google Gemini API. Private user documents are never sold or used to train public models.

3. Cloud Sync, Authentication & Google Play Billing

  • Authentication: Supports Google Sign-In and Firebase Anonymous Authentication for instant onboarding.
  • Cloud Sync: Documents synced across devices are stored in your private Firebase Firestore and Cloud Storage path (users/{userId}/documents) protected by per-user security rules.
  • In-App Purchases: Storage tiers and Prepaid Global AI Wallet passes ($99–$999 USD one-time or monthly plans) are processed strictly through Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store credit card numbers.

4. Data Deletion & User Control

You can toggle "On-Device OCR Only" anytime in Settings or delete any document directly from the Smart Vault / Document Inspector, which removes it from your local SQLite Room vault and your synced cloud storage.

5. Contact Us

For privacy inquiries or data deletion requests, contact: support@docusnap.ai
